# Landing page wireframe

Open [`landing-page-wireframe.html`](./landing-page-wireframe.html) directly in
a browser. It is a grey-box wireframe for the landing page, not a final visual
design or an implementation.

## Regions

1. **Identity and orientation.** A wordmark and intentionally minimal
   navigation.
2. **Hero.** The product idea, a short explanation, a future primary action,
   and an invented illustration or example.
3. **How it works.** The three-step journey: start with a place, read the
   story, and look closer.
4. **The principle.** A clear statement that disagreement remains visible and
   claims lead back to their sources.
5. **Footer.** A small place for project status or a future entry point.

The layout collapses to one column below 860px and stacks the hero content below
560px. The annotations explain the information hierarchy and the boundaries from
the statement of work.

## Marking invented material

The review wireframe uses two levels of labeling:

- The page-level banner says, **"Draft — examples on this page are invented
  and the copy is not approved for public release."**
- Any example that could be mistaken for a real place, person, quote, record,
  or testimony gets a visible label beside it. The illustration region uses
  **"Illustrative example — invented for this draft."**

These labels are text, not colour-only indicators or hover content. If a future
version includes a fabricated quote, it should be labeled **"Invented sample
passage — not a real account."** A fabricated person should be labeled
**"Invented contributor — not a real person."**

## Approval boundary

This wireframe contains draft wording only. The landing page must not be
deployed publicly until the sponsor approves the copy. It must not name an
organisation or present invented examples as real testimony.
