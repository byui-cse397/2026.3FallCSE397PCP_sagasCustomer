## What is out of scope for this version

- Organisation names.
- Real testimony or invented material presented as real.
- Scores, counts, rankings, or any signal that the site is ready to use.
- Mailing-list forms, analytics, changelog, authentication, or public
  deployment configuration.

## Marking invented material

Any content a reader could mistake for a real place, person, quote, record, or
testimony must be marked beside that content. Labels must be visible text; do
not rely on colour, italics, icons, hover text, tooltips, or the page-level
banner alone.

- **Illustrative example, invented** for the corner shop or any sample place.
- **Invented sample passage - not a real story** for a fabricated quotation or
  passage.
- **Invented contributor - not a real person** for a fabricated contributor.

## One-line reasons for the demo

- **Why first:** People need the reason to care before they need the mechanics.
- **Why the digital-plaque image:** It makes the purpose concrete without
  promising that the site is already open.
- **Why keep every story visible:** The idea is not to choose one official
  version of a place.
- **Why show sources beside stories:** Readers should be able to see where a
  story came from and decide what to make of it.
- **Why say the site is not open:** It prevents the page from sounding like a
  live service and sets an honest expectation for early conversations.
- **Why the corner shop:** It is the project's worked example, and labelling it
  protects readers from mistaking the draft for testimony.

### De4 draft — Where a story belongs

**Heading**

> Sagas helps a story stay connected to a place, while an archive can keep it
> permanently.

**Copy**

> Sagas is built around places, so it can turn up the people, records, and
> leads connected to a story. If you want material kept permanently, an archive
> may be the right place to donate it. We plan for contributors to choose what
> stays public and to be able to take something out of public view.

This is planned product behavior. It does not name an archive or provide a
donation path.

### De5 draft — How stories are looked after

**Heading**

> Memories stay visible as memories, with the sources behind them.

**Copy**

> Sagas is designed to keep each memory connected to its source. When people
> remember something differently, the different versions stay visible with
> their own sources. Contributors choose what is public.

This describes intended direction and does not add a live feature claim.
