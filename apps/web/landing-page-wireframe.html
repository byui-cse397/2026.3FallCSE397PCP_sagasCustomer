Why Sagas exists

When people move on, stories can disappear with them.

Sagas keeps the memories connected to a place, so a person who comes looking later can still find them. Every story stays visible with the records and sources that help someone understand where it came from.

Where Sagas stands

This is an early project, and the site is not open yet.

Sagas is being built by student teams at Idaho universities. We are sharing the idea and the work in progress while we learn from the people who remember these places.

Questions or thoughts? Write to info@civum.io.
