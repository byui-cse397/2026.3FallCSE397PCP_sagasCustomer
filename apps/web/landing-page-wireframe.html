Sagas Project status or future entry point

1Identity and orientation

The wordmark establishes the product without naming an organisation. Navigation stays intentionally small until there is an approved destination to link to.

2Hero and first action

The headline states the central idea. Supporting copy explains the product without promising a feature that does not exist. Any illustration or example must be marked invented.

3Product explanation

Three steps make the reading journey understandable before the full product is available. This section should remain useful without a map, sign-up form, or API.

4Trust principle

Disagreement stays visible and claims remain attributable. The final wording needs sponsor approval before deployment.
